What do these columns mean?
  • Total docid: all docids in the sales group that are callable on 216 db_paidcontracts.tbl_b2b_panindia_data today (2 Nov 2026), with the same query: (callable_flag = 1 OR paid <> 0) AND mask = 0 AND freeze = 0 AND closedown_flag NOT IN (1,9,10). VFL included. Sales group from the shared b2b master (same for every dashboard). Shown when grouping by sales group or vertical.
  • Vertical: one vertical per sales group, the same as the 3Cr dashboard (so a vertical always holds whole sales groups, and Total docid / Coverage % add up correctly). Groups without a vertical there show as "No vertical".
  • Coverage %: Docid with reviews and rating ÷ Total docid. Both come from the same day and query, so it is never above 100%.
  • Docid with reviews and rating: unique docids from the rating & review table (with Source = Justdial / Google / Others: only docids with a rating or reviews in that source; with Source = All: docids with a rating or reviews in any source) (120, india_snapshot.tbl_rating_review_master) in the row that pass all filters and are callable: on 216 db_paidcontracts.tbl_b2b_panindia_data, (callable_flag = 1 OR paid <> 0) AND mask = 0 AND freeze = 0 AND closedown_flag NOT IN (1,9,10). Paid docids: of those, paid = 1 on the same b2b row (216 tbl_b2b_panindia_data, 2 Nov). Every other paid value (0, 2 = expired, …) counts as non-paid.
  • With rating: docids whose rating for the chosen source is above 0. With reviews: docids whose review count is above 0. Counted separately.
  • Avg rating: simple average of the chosen source's rating over docids with a rating (0.1–5). Docids with no rating, and ratings above 5 (bad data), are left out.
  • Avg reviews: average review count over docids with reviews (sum of their reviews ÷ With reviews). Docids with 0 reviews are left out.
  • Median rating: the middle rating among docids with a rating (0.1–5).
  • A median marked ≈ is an estimate: the true value lies in the same review range (only in the HTML Drop version, above 99 reviews).
  • Median reviews: the middle review count among docids with reviews (half have fewer, half more). Not pulled up by a few very large listings, unlike the average.
  • Rating bands / Review bands: how many docids fall in each band. Each docid is in exactly one rating band and one review band, so each set adds up to Docids.
  • Sales group comes from the shared b2b master (snapshot 2 Nov 2026), the same for every dashboard: the 3Cr table's group (25 Sep tie-break) for docids already in it; other docids are looked up in the live tbl_sales_group_tagged (same tie-break), then hot_category → Sales_Manual_Group_mapping. Docids still without a named group ("Unmapped", 174,981 TAM docids, mostly blank in the source table) are left out of this dashboard.
