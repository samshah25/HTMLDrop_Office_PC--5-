Data sources & methodology
Sources: Monetizable TAM = distinct docids in db_paidcontracts.tbl_b2b_panindia_data (server 216) WHERE (callable_flag = 1 OR paid <> 0) AND mask = 0 AND freeze = 0 AND closedown_flag NOT IN (1,9,10), minus VFL docids: 3,08,79,215 − 1,19,363 = 3,07,59,852. Paid = those docids with paid = 1 (5,39,158); Penetration = Paid ÷ TAM. Every number is a distinct business (docid). Sales group = one per docid, the same for every dashboard (shared b2b master): the 25 Sep 2026 tie-break of vertical_owner.tbl_sales_group_tagged (server 216; most categories wins, a tie goes to the more common group, then alphabetical) for docids that existed then; newer docids get the same rule on the live table, then their hot_category → Sales_Manual_Group_mapping. No group, or a group outside this dashboard's list = Unmapped (1,74,834).
Data as of: TAM, Paid and sales groups - pulled 2 Nov 2026; VFL - 2 Nov 2026; LBC - 24 Sep 2026; Revenue - FY26 file. Revenue = official NCT & Sales Group wise Revenue FY26.xlsx, matched by sales_group name - ARPU in Target Builder = revenue ÷ paid.
VFL (Verified Forever Listing) docids excluded: every docid in db_paidcontracts.tbl_daily_paid_contracts_dump WHERE vfl_flag = 1 (1,23,197 docids, pulled 2 Nov 2026). 1,19,363 of them were in the TAM query, all with paid = 1, so they are removed from both TAM and Paid - a VFL business never counts as unconverted opportunity.
Dominant sales_group tie-break (when a docid has 2+ sales_group tags at the same category-count): resolve non-tied docids first, then for ties pick whichever tag is already more common among the non-tied resolutions, alphabetical as last resort. The dashboard lists 2,788 sales groups; 255 of them have 0 TAM because they never win this resolution for any docid - real tags that always lose to a more common competitor, not a data error.
