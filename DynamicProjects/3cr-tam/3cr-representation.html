Overview

All data is Monetizable TAM: callable or paid businesses (not masked, frozen or closed). Click any box to open it in Target Builder. Monetizable TAM = distinct docids in db_paidcontracts.tbl_b2b_panindia_data (server 216) WHERE (callable_flag = 1 OR paid <> 0) AND mask = 0 AND freeze = 0 AND closedown_flag NOT IN (1,9,10), minus VFL docids: 3,11,29,461 − 1,19,418 = 3,10,10,043. Paid = those docids with paid = 1 (5,48,093); Penetration = Paid ÷ TAM. Every number is a distinct business (docid). Sales group = one per docid, the same for every dashboard (shared b2b master): the 25 Sep 2026 tie-break of vertical_owner.tbl_sales_group_tagged (server 216; most categories wins, a tie goes to the more common group, then alphabetical) for docids that existed then; newer docids get the same rule on the live table, then their hot_category → Sales_Manual_Group_mapping. No group, or a group outside this dashboard's list = Unmapped (1,66,200).
Data as of: TAM, Paid and sales groups - pulled 26 Oct 2026; VFL - 26 Oct 2026; LBC - 24 Sep 2026; Revenue - FY26 file.

VFL (Verified Forever Listing) docids excluded from every TAM, Paid and Penetration number on this page. VFL (Verified Forever Listing) docids excluded: every docid in db_paidcontracts.tbl_daily_paid_contracts_dump WHERE vfl_flag = 1 (1,23,247 docids, pulled 26 Oct 2026). 1,19,418 of them were in the TAM query, all with paid = 1, so they are removed from both TAM and Paid - a VFL business never counts as unconverted opportunity.
