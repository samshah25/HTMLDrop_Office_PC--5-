JDRR vs JD Trust

Upgrade to JDRR

When a JD Trust holder reaches a 3.5★+ rating, offer JDRR at a discount. That makes two sales from one business.

How to sell it
🖼️

Let merchants see and hold it

Show the physical certificate at expos, trade fairs and merchant meets, so owners can see exactly what they'd get. Give resellers and field sales a demo certificate to carry to every pitch.

Fixes "don't know what the product is"
🤝

Bundle it with partner products

Partner with software and service providers merchants already buy from. For example, Petpooja could offer JD Trust with its restaurant POS. The partner earns a share, and the merchant gets the certificate at the point of purchase.

Reaches merchants at the moment they buy
💬

WhatsApp and email campaigns

Run campaigns aimed at storefront businesses that are new (listed 2025–26) or rated below 3.5★. Show a before-and-after of their listing with the badge, with a one-tap buy link.

50.8 L storefront businesses in this group
🏆

Recognition as a reward

Give JD Trust buyers a chance to be recognised. A lucky winner each month could be featured in a newspaper, on Justdial's social media or in other media. That gives merchants a reason to buy now.

Taps into the wish to be recognised
📱

Business influencers

Work with social media influencers who talk to small business owners (business tips, local business and trade creators). Have them show the certificate and explain what it does for customer trust.

Builds awareness: merchants who had seen it were more likely to consider it (33% of calls had seen it and would consider, vs 24% who hadn't seen it)
Bundles resellers can sell

Field visits showed a certificate on its own can look like decoration, and merchants want more customers. These four bundles, based on the reseller offerings deck, pair the certificate with something that brings customers.

Digital Dukaan All-in-One QR

One QR at the counter: customers pay, leave a review and see the shop's Justdial page.

  • Payment solutions (UPI, cards)
  • Review prompt after every payment
  • Listing and catalogue on scan
  • Verified Seal and counter standee
For: counter shops (kirana, chemist, bakery, salon) and new listings with no reviews
Every payment asks for a review, so the rating grows

Rating Rescue

A paid 90-day service that helps the business collect genuine ratings and reviews from its real customers.

  • QR review standee
  • WhatsApp review requests
  • Help replying to reviews
  • Once rated 3.5★ or higher, JDRR is offered as a paid upgrade
For: the 1.94 Cr below the bar: rated under 3.5★ or with no rating yet
A paid service now, and a JDRR sale later

New Business Launch Kit

JD Trust plus a week at the top of search, so a new business is trusted and seen from its first week.

  • JD Trust certificate and digital badge
  • Premium Listing for 7 days
  • Mobile and website banners for 7 days
  • Report on calls and views at the end
For: new listings, ~3,200 a day in 2026, starting with no rating and no reviews
Sold during onboarding, when trust matters most

JDRR + Banner

The JDRR certificate in the shop, plus the business's banner on competitors' listings online.

  • JD Rating Certificate (framed)
  • Banner on competitor listings (mobile or website)
  • Digital JDRR badge for WhatsApp and Instagram
  • Results report at the end
For: businesses rated 3.5★ or higher, especially ones with close competitors nearby
Gives the certificate a clear business benefit
