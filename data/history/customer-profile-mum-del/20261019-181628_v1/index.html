What is a valence score?

A valence score measures how positive or negative a customer's reply is towards the agent, on a scale from −2 (clearly negative) to +2 (clearly positive). A call is one conversation (2,060 in total); a turn is one reply by one speaker (34,575 customer turns).

−2Refuses or complains“Humein yeh service nahi leni”
“Baar baar call karke pareshan kar rahe ho”
−1Objects or puts off“Yeh bahut mehenga hai”
“Abhi busy hoon, baad mein call karna”
0Neutral“Sector 24, Noida”
“Mera kapde ka kaam hai”
+1Agrees“Theek hai, bataiye”
“Haan, samajh gaya”
+2Asks the price or commits“Package kitne ka hai?”
“Kal 4 baje aa jaiye”

How is it calculated?

1
Score every customer turn by matching its words to the scale above.
e.g. “Package kitne ka hai?” → +2
2
Average the turns in each call.
e.g. turns +1, 0, 0, −1, +2 → (1 + 0 + 0 − 1 + 2) ÷ 5 = +0.4
3
Average the calls in each city (each call counts once). Stage scores use only the turns right after that stage.
e.g. calls +0.4, 0.0, +0.5 → +0.300

Dialect and language variations were accounted for: Mumbai and Delhi were scored with separate word lists, so regional wording is scored on the same basis in both cities.
