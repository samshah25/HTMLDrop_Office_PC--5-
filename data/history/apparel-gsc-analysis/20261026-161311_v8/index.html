Category vs company search split

  • Category search = URL has an ncatid (a JD results page).
  • Company search = URL has a docid (a business listing and its tabs).
  • Both ids (6,449 rows) = category search.
  • Brand + generic categories are a subset of category search (ncatid) – they add up to the category search %. Company search is not split.
  • Brand category: category name has a brand (Zudio Stores, Shoe Dealers Bata). Generic: the rest (Readymade Garment Retailers).

How the numbers are made

Search buckets (GSC)

  • Brand / business name: search has the page's own shop name, or a chain brand (Zudio, Bata, Peter England).
  • Chain brands: from the b2b master – shops flagged as a chain; the word most outlets share.
  • Info: timings, contact, address, reviews, photos, price, offers, jobs.
  • Category + near me: "near me", "nearby", "nearest".
  • Category + location: a city, area or pincode.
  • Category / product only: only product or category words.
  • Order: a search gets the first bucket that fits, in this order.

Search types

  • Category search: ncatid set. Company search: docid set (ncatid wins if both).
  • Brand category: category name has a chain brand; else generic.
  • Intent view: company search + brand categories together.

Measures

  • CTR = clicks ÷ impressions. Position = Google rank, 1 = top, weighted by impressions.
  • CAGR = average yearly growth.

Checks

  • Row counts match your count query exactly (17,978,537 rows).
  • Every split adds up to the totals – see the line at the bottom.
