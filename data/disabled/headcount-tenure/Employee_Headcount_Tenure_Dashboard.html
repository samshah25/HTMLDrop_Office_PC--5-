🧭 Quick Dashboard Guide & How to Use

🔍

Filters

  • 🗓️ Period: Pick MTD (active month), Monthly, Quarterly (Q1–Q4), or Yearly.
  • 🏢 Branch: Select individual cities or All 11 Cities for Pan-India.
  • 🌐 Location: Toggle between Main (Metro HQ), Remote, or Both.
  • 👤 EmpType & Team: JDA, ME, or TME (with specific call queues).
📊

Metrics & Logic

  • Active Headcount: FTE capacity (active_days / 26). Decimals = mid-month join/exit.
  • Total Exits: Employees whose leave date (dol) fell in this period.
  • New Joiners: Staff whose join date (doj) was in this period.
  • Growth: +% / -% vs. previous month (MoM), quarter (QtQ), or year (YoY).
📑

Tabs & Navigation

  • 1. Headcount: Branch & team summary. Click any city to view its teams!
  • 2. Tenure: Split across 6 experience stages (<6M to 5+Y).
  • 3. Drill-Down: Search roster by name/ID, view exits, and export to Excel.
  • ⚡ Fast Cache: Pre-cached in browser for instant ~200ms reload.
📈

Graph Meanings

  • Branch Bar Chart: Gray = Prior, Blue = Current. Taller blue = growth; shorter blue = attrition.
  • Trend Line Chart: 6–12 month headcount path showing hiring momentum & seasonal peaks.
  • Tenure Donut: Visual % split of workforce across the 6 experience stages.
  • Stacked Bar Chart: 100% normalized view showing junior (<6M) vs. veteran (3+Y) mix per city.
