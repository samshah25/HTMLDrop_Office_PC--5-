01 | The challengeObserved pattern, to be confirmed with data

Overlapping channel coverage is creating duplicate merchant engagement

Sales and resellers operate across overlapping merchant pools. As a result, merchants already being pursued by Sales can also enter reseller campaign lists, leading to duplicate outreach and unclear channel ownership.

What the data does not cover
  • The lead log starts on 22 Oct 2026, so "reseller only" means no Sales calling queue entry since then. It does not mean new to Justdial.
  • "Given to Sales as well" means the lead was placed in the Sales calling queue. It does not show that Sales actively worked it.
  • The 109 leads with a Sales appointment already scheduled carry no reseller agency code in the log. They are counted as reseller leads on the campaign owner's confirmation, and 3 of them appear in the partner cases.
  • A Sales appointment after the reseller got the lead shows the order of events, not who deserves the credit.
  • Not in the data: reseller contacts and conversions, Sales calls without an appointment, deal values.
03 | The diagnosisHypotheses, to be validated against the evidence

Four structural gaps are limiting reseller channel productivity

The current operating model creates overlap in merchant coverage while providing different levels of lead visibility, ownership and management support across channels.

01

Overlapping merchant coverage

Sales and resellers operate across the same geographies and merchant segments, without clearly defined channel boundaries.

Underlying issueMarket allocation
Evidence: 3,221 of the 3,372 hot leads went to a reseller, across 20 cities, and 1,806 of them were given to Sales as well
02

Limited lead differentiation

Reseller campaign lists may include merchants already present in the Sales pipeline, reducing incremental opportunities.

Underlying issueLead sourcing and allocation
Evidence: 1,495 of the 3,221 reseller leads were with Sales before the reseller got them. 1,144 went to Sales afterwards
03

Unclear lead ownership

There is no sufficiently clear mechanism to identify existing Sales engagement before leads are assigned to resellers.

Underlying issueOwnership and attribution
Evidence: 109 reseller leads already had a Sales appointment scheduled. 53 of 84 Sales appointments were set after the reseller got the lead. 13 leads went to two agencies
04

Uneven management support

Sales does push sales: its teams call merchants and follow up rigorously, backed by structured performance reviews. Resellers do not call or follow up with the same rigour, and they have limited dedicated support from Justdial.

Underlying issueChannel enablement
Evidence: field observation. Reseller call and follow-up data is pending

04 | The proposed modelProposed operating rules, pending approval

Build a structured reseller ecosystem through clear ownership and dedicated support

A differentiated market strategy, combined with dedicated reseller management, can help reduce channel overlap, improve conversion opportunities and drive incremental business.

Today
Overlapping merchant coverage, no ownership check.
Proposed
Every merchant has one channel owner.
Pillar 1 - Market and lead ownership

Create clearly defined markets for each channel

Lead filtering
Exclude merchants with active Sales engagement before reseller allocation. 90-day window: starting hypothesis
Lead ownership
Establish clear ownership and attribution rules.
Territory allocation
Define Sales and reseller geographies based on existing coverage and market opportunity.
Conflict resolution
Define a common process for resolving disputed merchant ownership.
Pillar 2 - Dedicated reseller management

Introduce regional management to drive reseller productivity

Sales does push sales, with regular calls and rigorous follow-up. Resellers do not, so each region needs a dedicated manager to bring the same discipline to the reseller channel.

Lead management
Review lead allocation and follow-up progress.
Performance monitoring
Track activity, conversions and pending opportunities.
Sales enablement
Support pitch training, product knowledge and objection handling.
Relationship management
Act as the single point of contact for resellers.
Conflict resolution
Coordinate with Sales on ownership-related disputes.
Sequencing: lead-level ownership checks come first, with geographical exclusivity where commercially appropriate. A geographical split alone is not sufficient: some markets may not have enough reseller opportunity, and Sales coverage is uneven.
Operating cadence to consider: weekly lead reviews, a shared reseller scorecard, periodic training and a defined escalation path.
Expected outcomes

Higher lead productivity

More reseller effort directed towards genuinely incremental merchant opportunities.

Clear channel ownership

Reduced overlap and fewer disputes between Sales and resellers.

Improved reseller performance

Stronger follow-up discipline, better conversion and higher engagement.

Better merchant experience

Coordinated communication and a more consistent sales journey.

Intended outcomes, not projections: improvements will be reported only against an agreed baseline and methodology.
