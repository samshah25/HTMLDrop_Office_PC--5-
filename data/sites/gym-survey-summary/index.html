GYM PRIMARY RESEARCH - SURVEY CALLS

What non-branded gyms told us
i
Source: JD_Gyms_Survey_Tracker (3 sheets: paid, never paid, paid before - expired), connected calls only. Free-text answers grouped into the tracker's own options; promotion channels can be several per gym. Never-paid 'Priority Tag' is the same pre-filled text on every row, so it is left out. Full tables and the cleaned call list (no phone numbers): Gym_Survey_Analysis.xlsx.

449 calls, 03 Nov - 13 Nov 2026; 249 connected. Gyms split by their Justdial paid status. % = of gyms that answered each question.

Click a card to see only that group (All = the three side by side). Click any bar, % or number to see the gyms behind it and what they said; click a finding heading to jump to its chart.
Showing: All gyms - the three groups side by side

Key findings

  1. Not enough leads ↓
    • 47% of gyms said no, 25% only somewhat, 28% yes
    • Paid-now gyms are the most satisfied: 47% yes
    • Never paid: 26% yes; paid before, expired: only 11% yes
  2. Promotion: Instagram and agencies, weak results ↓
    • Instagram used by 80% of gyms, referrals by 61%
    • 51% run Meta / other ads, mostly through an agency (median Rs 4,000 a month, n = 126)
    • 21% say social media is effective
  3. Enquiries tracked on paper ↓
    • Register or memory: 54%; Excel / WhatsApp: 37%
    • 7% use a CRM; 11% use any software to convert enquiries
  4. No time to follow up ↓
    • 53% cannot follow up every lead
    • Median gym: 140 members (n = 248), 32 leads a month (n = 249), 24% convert (n = 248)
  5. Daily operations are manual ↓
    • Attendance in a register: 44% of gyms
    • Stock in a register or not kept: 92%
    • Crowding seen by eye or CCTV: 90%
  6. Renewals get missed ↓
    • 41% sometimes or often miss reminding a member
    • About 3 follow-ups to renew one member (median, n = 248)
    • 13% use reminder software; the rest call or WhatsApp by hand
  7. Hardly any software ↓
    • Zenoti 3%, other gym CRMs 6%, Justdial named as a lead source by 7%
    • Open space for a simple lead + renewal tool
