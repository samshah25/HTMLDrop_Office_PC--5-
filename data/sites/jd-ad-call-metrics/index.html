How each type of ad is measured

Judge every ad only on its own job.

🎯
One job per adBe remembered (brand ad) or get an action now (action ad)
⚖️
Judge it on that job onlyDon't judge a TV ad on clicks, or a search ad on reach
🧪
Prove it with a comparisonPeople or cities that saw it vs those that didn't

3Four ways to prove any ad worked, without a survey

🔎Search jump

Google Trends for "justdial" in ad cities: 2 weeks before, during, 2 weeks after vs non-ad cities. Same data as Users → Searches.

🚶Coming on their own

App opens and visits not from a paid ad (typed the name, opened the app). Same metric as Users → App usage.

🗺️City test

Run in some cities, not in similar ones. The gap in installs, searches and calls = what the ad did.

📞On the calls

Calls mentioning the ad ("IPL pe dekha"), and fewer sales-call hang-ups after a big campaign (Merchants → Sales calls).

Why weight
  • A bad video seen by 50,000 people matters more than one review
  • Weighted score = (weight of good − weight of bad) ÷ total weight
Example
  • 100 reviews: 60 good, 30 bad, 10 neutral → +30
  • Add 1 bad video, 50,000 views (weight 50) → (60 − 80) ÷ 150 = −13
  • One video turns the month negative
