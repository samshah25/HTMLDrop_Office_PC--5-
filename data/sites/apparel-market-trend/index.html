The trend, one question at a time

How many small clothing businesses are there?

Dark = in villages, light = in towns and cities. The number on top is the total. Source: all businesses · villages · towns and cities (MoSPI ASUSE, raw data).

How much does one business earn in a month?

"Earnings" = what the business makes after paying for cloth, thread and other materials (before paying wages and rent). Light bars show the same amount in 2021-22 rupees, i.e. after taking out price rise, so you can compare what it actually buys. Source: earnings per business (MoSPI ASUSE, raw data). Price index: MoSPI CPI, base 2012 (2025 page shown; the build reads 2017 – 2025).

How many of them have paid staff?

A business with at least one regular paid worker. These are the closest thing to a "proper" garment business; the rest are run by the owner alone or with family. Source: businesses by type: with paid staff / owner-run (MoSPI ASUSE, raw data).

How many people work in them?

Counts owners, family members who help, and paid staff, full-time and part-time. Source: workers, all and women (MoSPI ASUSE, raw data).

Are clothing makers a growing part of all small businesses?

Out of every 100 small businesses in India (shops, workshops, services), how many make clothes. Textile makers (spinning, weaving) shown for comparison. Source: clothing, textile and all businesses (MoSPI ASUSE, raw data).

How big are they?

Size uses the government's MSME limits on investment and yearly sales. Available for 2025 only. Source: MSME size split (MoSPI ASUSE, raw data).
