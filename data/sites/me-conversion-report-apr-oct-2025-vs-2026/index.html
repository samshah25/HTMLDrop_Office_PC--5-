Where the drop sits

  • Add-ons now booked inside the main deal: add-on products (JDRR, JD Trusted, Mobile banner, Competitors and Category Banners, JD Verified) were mostly booked as their own deal rows; from Feb–Apr 2026 they are booked inside the main product's deal row. Add-on deal rows on their own: 27,682 → 11,932. The sales dashboard counts each deal row as a deal, so this alone takes 3.4 pts off conversion, more than the whole fall (-1.6 pts). Conversion on everything else: 24.2% → 26.0%. RV3yr of all the deals in the conversion: ₹396.8 cr → ₹512.5 cr (+29%). Details in the next section.
  • Delhi: conversion 38.8% → 31.3%, with appointments given +23.4%. Pan India conversion without Delhi: 27.5% → 27.2%. Delhi booked the most add-ons as their own deal rows; without those rows, Delhi's conversion (appointment data): 29.9% → 28.8%. Details in the Delhi section below.
  • Appointments MEs log for themselves (TME team "BDE", the TME code is the ME's own): from Feb 2026, about 1,500 a month (1.9% of appointments given). Counted as appointments given; none ends Dealclosed and none carries a TME team, so none can add a deal to the conversion. Conversion without them: 29.7% → 28.7%.
  • Attended %: 83.3% → 79.5%; without the self-logged and B2B appointments 83.3% → 80.4%. Appointments allocated a day or more ahead (self-logged and B2B left out): 31.5% → 36.3% of appointments given; their attended % is 60.8%, against 90.6% for same-day appointments.
  • Newer TMEs: 4,842–6,021 TMEs gave appointments a month (2025: 4,221–4,562), 57–63% of them within their first year (2025: 51–55%). Within the teams, this shift accounts for -0.6 pts of the conversion change; the ME tenure mix for +0.1.
  • ME count: MEs a month 1,604 → 1,565 while appointments given (B2B and self-logged left out) rose 4.9%: 44.6 → 48.0 appointments given per ME a month. With each ME-load band at its 2025 conversion, this shift alone moves conversion by -0.1 pts. Delhi: 287 → 267 MEs a month, 47.5 → 56.2 appointments given per ME; conversion of its MEs with 80 or more appointments a month 41.5% → 32.4%, with 40–59 39.2% → 35.6%. Details in "Appointments given per ME".
  • Hot Data: conversion 20.2% → 19.0%; August 20.2% → 18.1%, October 22.6% → 16.8%.

How these are counted

Appointments given and conversion are the sales dashboard's "ME Appointment Given" and "ME Conversion % On Given" (JDS-ME), April–October of each year; November is left out (part month).

Conversion = deals closed that carry a TME team ÷ appointments given, by month. Attended = Dealclosed, Follow Up or Not Interested.

TME team, tenure and the self-logged appointments come from the appointment data, which matches the sales dashboard's totals within 0.1–0.4% per branch.

MEs a month = MEs with one or more appointments given that month (B2B and self-logged left out), each counted once, in the branch where they had most of them; a deal counts for the ME on the deal (every such deal has its ME's appointments that month).

ME load (April–October 2026): per HR present day; ME = HR type ME, not Corporate, with present days; appointments = every appointment the ME closed with an outcome (appointments given plus the same-day “customer not available”, fake, wrong-details and similar ones); October's missing outcomes taken from the ME allocation system; deals = the ME's deal rows (deal credit), all channels; RV3yr = the RV3yr of those rows.

Distance: appointment pincode (ME allocation system) placed at its centre (Department of Posts pincode boundaries); the ME's usual area = the middle of the same ME's placed appointments that month (median latitude and longitude); straight-line km. Met by the ME = Follow Up or Not Interested, of the appointments that did not end Dealclosed. Against the same ME = the rate minus the same ME's rate that month for appointments of the same timing (same day / allocated ahead).
