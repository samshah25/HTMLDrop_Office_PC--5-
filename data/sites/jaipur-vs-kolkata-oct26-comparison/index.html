1Why Jaipur is behind, and what to act on

Why Jaipur is behind

Jaipur is not behind everywhere: its Main City grew +22% against Kolkata's −3%. The gap is Remote City, plus two gaps across the whole branch: new JDAs and ECS months.

  1. Remote City: 24 points to Kolkata, while Jaipur is 12 points ahead in Main City. Remote grew +2% against +39%. Per manday in Remote, Jaipur's Super Cat brings 72% of Kolkata's RV 3-yr, its JDAs 57% and its MEs 40%; Super Cat and Hot Data contracts are 76%74% and 75%74% of Kolkata's size.
  2. Super Cat is small for its load and works only paid contracts: 47 TMEs against 150, 7.4 renewals due per TME against 2.0. Per TME it keeps the same pace and conversion, but spends 6% of its trunk duration on non-paid contracts against 47%.
  3. MEs are under-fed, not under-performing: 41 appointments per ME against 54, closed at the same 33%; Super Cat sets 15 a month per ME against 32. In Main City, Jaipur's MEs close as many deals per ME (14.3 against 14.5).
  4. New JDAs do not reach a first deal: 69% of those who joined after 15 July have none (Kolkata 24%), in Main City and Remote alike; 65% of JDA leavers go within three months.
  5. ECS customers pay for fewer months: 8.5 against 11.4 (company 9.2), in every team and on both sides; more come back through Bounce or Retention as a new deal (9.3% against 0.6%).
  6. Too few people: TME + JDA mandays rose 6.1% against 35.5%.

Not the problem: ME conversion, Super Cat output per TME, the Hot Data team (ahead) and Main City.

What to act on

  1. Build Remote Super Cat capacity: more Super Cat TMEs on Remote paid contracts, and a share of Remote non-paid leads as in Kolkata; from hiring, or some of the 28 experienced Hot Data TMEs (watching Hot Data, a strength today). About ₹27 lakh a month.
  2. Raise appointments per ME from 41 towards 54, mostly through action 1; MEs have room (Kolkata's busiest close 38%). About ₹71 lakh (overlaps with 1).
  3. Fix a new JDA's first 90 days: pair each with an experienced JDA, a first-deal target within 30–60 days, a weekly look at presentations against deals, and an exit review for early leavers; start with the 56 who joined after 15 July and have no deal yet. About ₹15 lakh.
  4. Remote pricing and Remote JDA output: Remote contract size, and Remote JDAs towards the company's Remote rate. About ₹23 lakh of RV 3-yr, and ₹41 lakh₹44 lakh of contract value.
  5. Keep ECS contracts running: after a bounce, recover on the running contract rather than re-sign; start with JDA ECS deals (7.5 months). About ₹27 lakh for each extra month; ₹20 lakh at the company's 9.2.
  6. Hire where it works: Jaipur's new Hot Data TMEs already set more appointments than Kolkata's (0.37 against 0.22 a manday); scale JDA hiring after action 3.

Amounts are a month, at Jaipur's own October rates, and overlap. Before setting targets: the original budget runs far above the budget on about 4% of Jaipur's deals, and attended % reads higher in Jaipur because its MEs record more non-meetings as Not Interested and fewer as Not Available.

2The gap

  • Growth: Jaipur's RV 3-yr grew 11.1% on October'25 and Kolkata's 25.6% (company 28.3%), a gap of 14.5 points.
  • Same deals, less value: both branches sell 0.18 deals per TME + JDA manday, but a Kolkata manday brings ₹5,454 of RV 3-yr and a Jaipur manday ₹4,202.
  • By team: Super Cat and JDA make up the gap (about 12 and 10 points); Kolkata's Hot Data team gives 9 back.
  • By lead type: Kolkata's Super Cat team spends 47% of its trunk duration on non-paid contracts; Jaipur's spends 6%. In Jaipur that work sits with the Hot Data team (98% of its trunk duration). So part of Kolkata's Super Cat figure is work that Hot Data does in Jaipur.

4The levers

1 · TMEs · Super Cat team

Super Cat: a small team with a heavy renewal load

  • Size: 47 TMEs against 150 (11% of Jaipur's people, 19% of Kolkata's).
  • Same pace and conversion: 0.94 appointments set per manday against 0.91, and 0.47 deals per appointment against 0.46. The gap in RV 3-yr per manday is all in value per deal: ₹24,889 against ₹31,977 (lever 5).
  • Renewal load: 346 one-year contracts fell due in October, 7.4 per Super Cat TME; Kolkata 312, 2.0 per TME. About half were renewed in both (50% and 54%).
  • Experience: of the TMEs with 12+ months in the company, Jaipur has 39 in Super Cat and 28 in Hot Data; Kolkata has 134 and 3.
  • Lead type: Kolkata's Super Cat team spends 47% of its trunk duration on non-paid contracts (Jaipur 6%); 38% of its appointments are on non-paid contracts (Jaipur 15%).

2 · MEs

MEs close the same share; they are given fewer appointments

  • Load: each Jaipur ME was given 41 appointments in October and each Kolkata ME 54; both closed 33%.
  • Funnel: per ME, Jaipur's 41 appointments became 38 attended and 13.6 Dealclosed; Kolkata's 54 became 44 and 17.8. Dealclosed is 33% and 33% of given, so the gap in deals per ME is the gap in appointments.
  • By the team that set them: Super Cat 15 against 32 per ME, Hot Data 16 against 11, the rest of the teams 10 against 11.
  • By lead type: non-paid contracts 22 against 30 per ME, paid contracts 14 against 18, paid expired 4 against 6. Most of the gap (8 of the 13.5 a month) is appointments on non-paid contracts.
  • Deal size: a Jaipur ME deal carries ₹26,626₹25,864 of contract value against ₹28,961₹28,371 (92%91%): 99%97% on paid contracts, 85% on non-paid contracts and 81%82% on paid expired.
  • Main City and Remote: Main City MEs close as many deals as Kolkata's (14.3 against 14.5 a month, from 41 appointments against 47). Remote MEs close 13.1 against 19.5 because they are given 41 appointments against 58; per manday, 0.55 deals against 0.87.
  • Room: Kolkata's 16 MEs given 70 or more appointments closed 38% of them; no Jaipur ME was given more than 68. Experience is the same: 81% and 82% of ME mandays come from MEs with 12+ months.

3 · JDAs

  • Conversion: 0.99 deals per 100 presentations in the first six months against 1.92; with 12+ months 7.8 against 7.2 (1–28 Oct).
  • Leavers: 65% of October's JDA leavers left within three months (Kolkata 37%). Experienced JDAs earn 96% of Kolkata's RV 3-yr per manday.
