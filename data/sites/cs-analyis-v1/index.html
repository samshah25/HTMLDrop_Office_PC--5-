    Customer grievance

    What the customer complained about, from customer_core_grievance.

    Contract type (last paid entry)
    What each column means
    DocidsEach docid (merchant) counted once, under its main grievance: the grievance sub-category on most of its calls, ignoring calls with no grievance (tie: latest call). A docid with no grievance on any call is under 0. No grievance.
    TicketsEach ticket counted once, under its main grievance, same rule as docids.
    CallsEach call counted once, under its own grievance.
    Summary supportsShare of the row's calls whose call_summary also mentions the problem (validation of the label).
    Section 3

    Agent resolution

    What the agent did on the call, from agent_action_taken.

    Contract type (last paid entry)
    What each bucket means
    A. Took action during the callThe agent changed something on the account during the call (settings, categories, blocks, profile, report sent).
    B. Request passed to another teamThe agent raised a ticket, forwarded a request or connected the merchant to another team during the call.
    C. Agent committed to a later actionThe agent said a change, check, escalation or callback would happen after the call.
    D. Merchant given steps to performThe agent told the merchant what to do (app steps, documents, profile edits, photos, contacting a team).
    E. Information given, no actionThe agent explained or checked something; no change, hand-off, commitment or step for the merchant.
    F. No action recordedNo conversation, or the issue was only noted.
    Tickets / docidsEach counted once, under the most decisive resolution reached on any of its calls (order A to F).
